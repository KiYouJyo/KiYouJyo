## 👋 About

I'm interested in the intersection of **urban planning, spatial computing and software engineering**.

My work spans two closely connected directions:

- 🏙️ **Urban & Spatial Research** — urban planning systems, spatial governance, GIS and urban morphology
- 🪟 **Software Engineering** — native Windows applications, spatial viewers, media tools and research software

I enjoy turning research workflows and everyday problems into focused, maintainable software.

## 🔬 Research & Spatial Computing

### [UrbanPlanningLab](https://github.com/KiYouJyo/UrbanPlanningLab)

Reusable and reproducible research infrastructure for urban planning and territorial spatial research.

Current interests include:

- China–Japan planning system comparison
- Urban and non-urban spatial governance
- Urban morphology and regeneration
- GIS-based spatial analysis
- Planning computation and reproducible research

### [ModernQGIS](https://github.com/KiYouJyo/ModernQGIS)

An experimental modern desktop shell and interaction architecture built on top of QGIS capabilities.

### [ReferenceCity](https://github.com/KiYouJyo/ReferenceCity)

A standardized synthetic city environment for reproducible spatial experiments and method validation.
